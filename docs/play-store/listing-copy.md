## Full description (≤4000 chars)

Hearth is a simple, fast place to talk to the people you actually want to
talk to — one-on-one or in a group, by text, voice, photo, or video.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Message one person or start a group, with replies, reactions, and edit/delete on anything you've sent
• Share photos, files, and voice messages right in the conversation
• Make one-on-one video calls, with a call log so you can see who called and when
• See when someone's online, when they were last active, and when they've read your message
• Search inside a conversation, or across all your chats at once
• Switch between light and dark themes, and between English and Bulgarian

YOUR DATA, YOUR CALL
• Permanently delete your account and everything tied to it, whenever you want — no waiting period
• Block or report anyone who's bothering you, right from the conversation

Hearth doesn't run ads, doesn't use tracking or analytics software, and
doesn't sell your information. See the full privacy policy for exactly
what's collected and why.
