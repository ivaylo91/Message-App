What we collect

Information we collect

Hearth collects only what it needs to run a messaging and calling service — nothing is collected for advertising, and Hearth does not use analytics or ad-tracking software of any kind.

CategoryWhat it includes
Account Email address and password (stored as a secure hash — Hearth never sees or stores your password in plain text).
Profile Display name, and — if you choose to add them — a username, phone number, and profile photo.
Messages & media Text messages, photos, files, and voice messages you send, and who they were sent to.
Calls Call log entries (who, when, how long). Call audio/video is streamed directly between devices where possible; see "Who we share it with" for how relaying works when it isn't.
Presence & activity Online status, last-active time, and read receipts, visible to people you're chatting with.
Device A push-notification token, used only to deliver notifications to your device.
Purpose

How we use it

  • Run the app — deliver your messages, calls, and media to the people you send them to.
  • Notify you — send push notifications for new messages and incoming calls. Notification previews never include your message content, only that something arrived.
  • Keep your account secure — authentication, session management, and password resets.
  • Keep the service running — operational logs used to diagnose and fix problems.
Third parties

Who we share it with

Hearth doesn't sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers or data brokers. A small number of infrastructure providers process data strictly to run the service on our behalf:

Supabase
Database, authentication, file storage, and realtime messaging infrastructure (hosted in the EU).
Google Firebase Cloud Messaging
Delivers push notifications to your device. Only a delivery token is shared — never message content.
Cloudflare
Relays encrypted call audio/video only when a direct connection between callers isn't possible. Doesn't store call content.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

How long

Data retention

Your messages and account data are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, messages, media, call history, and push-notification tokens as soon as you confirm the request — there's no separate waiting period.

In your control

Protection

Security

  • Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) between your device and our servers.
  • Your session is stored in your device's secure Keychain/Keystore, not in ordinary app storage.
  • Database-level access controls restrict who can read or write which data on the backend.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Age

Children's privacy

Hearth is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
